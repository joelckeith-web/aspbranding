YOUR GOOGLE PROFILE (paste into Claude with screenshots attached)

Claude often can't open a live Google Business Profile, so take screenshots of yours and paste them in with this:

Here are screenshots of the live Google Business Profile for [business]. Using only what they show, list the category, services, hours, review count and rating, how recent the newest reviews are, owner replies, photos, and call and booking links. Mark anything you can't see as NOT VERIFIED. Add it to the audit.

----------------------------------------------------------------
THE THREE AI QUESTIONS (ask in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google)

Use a temporary or logged-out chat, and ask each one more than once.

1. Does AI get you right? Check the facts, the tone, and where any wrong information came from.

What do you know about [business] in [town, state]? Are they a good [trade] company?

2. Does AI name you? Keep your name out of the question, and copy the links each answer cites.

Who are the best [trade] companies in [town, state]? Who would you call, and why?

[Your customer's urgent problem, such as "My AC stopped working"] in [town, state]. Which company should I call?

Tip: build these from the way people actually search for your trade. Take your trade's biggest "near me" searches, such as "ac repair near me" or "kitchen remodel near me", and put your town in place of "near me."

3. Who is AI reading? Paste the links you copied into Claude with this:

Here are the pages an AI cited when asked who to call: [paste links]. Open each one. Give a table: page, type (directory, review site, best-of list, competitor, news, other), and whether it mentions [business]. If you can't open a page, mark it NOT READABLE and don't count it. If a list runs to more pages, check those too and note where the mention is, but count only the link I gave you. Then give me the count: X of Y readable pages mention [business]. Finish with the pages they could realistically get listed on, and how.

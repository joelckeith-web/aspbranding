Audit how one local business shows up on Google and in AI answers, then write a 30-day plan for it.

Business details: $ARGUMENTS

If any of these are missing from the details (business name, trade, town and state, website, main phone), ask me for them once before you start. Once you have them, work through every step below in order without stopping to ask me, and give me one report at the end.

RULES FOR EVERY STEP
Use only what you can open, and give the URL for each finding. If you can't open or confirm something, write NOT VERIFIED. Never guess, and never fill a gap from memory. If information comes from a site other than the business's own, such as Yelp or BBB, label it secondhand. Say when something is your judgment. Don't judge how the site looks on a phone; I'll check that myself. Before you write the report, go back over every finding and check it against the page you cited.

PART 1: THE AUDIT

Step 1, the website. Does it say what the business does, where, and how to call? Is there a real page for each service, and for the towns it serves? Does it answer the questions customers ask before they hire? Is there proof, such as reviews, licenses and real photos? Is anything blocking search engines? Spot-check up to 3 service pages and 3 town pages.

Step 2, outside listings. Do the name, address and phone number match the website on the listings you can find? If I gave you screenshots of the Google Business Profile, list the category, services, hours, review count and rating, how recent the newest reviews are, whether the owner replies, the photos, and the call and booking links. If I haven't, mark the Google profile NOT VERIFIED.

Step 3, what AI says about the business. Search for what you can find about the business by name in its town. Report whether the facts match the website, and where any mismatch comes from.

Step 4, the questions customers ask AI. People type short keywords into Google, but they ask AI full questions. Write 5 questions a real customer in this town might ask an AI assistant when they need this trade, in full sentences, the way a person talks. Include at least one urgent problem, one "who should I hire" question and one cost question. Leave the business name out. Then search each one the way a customer would, and list the businesses that come up and the pages they come from.

Step 5, the pages AI is reading. Open each page from Step 4. Give a table: page, type (directory, review site, best-of list, competitor, news, other), and whether it mentions the business. If you can't open a page, mark it NOT READABLE and don't count it. Give the count: X of Y readable pages mention the business.

Step 6, strengths and gaps. Using only what you verified, list what the business does well, where it falls short, the openings it could take, and what competitors are doing better. Give the source for each point.

PART 2: THE 30-DAY PLAN

Step 7, the plan. Using only what you found in Part 1, write a practical 30-day plan, one week at a time, that an owner could start on without hiring anyone. For each week, give 3 to 5 tasks. For each task, say what to do, why it matters for the questions in Step 4, and where it goes (the website, the Google profile, a specific listing, or reviews). Cover:
- What to build out: missing service pages, town pages or profile details.
- What information to put out: plain answers to the questions from Step 4, with real details like prices, timelines, service areas and proof.
- Where to get listed: the directories and lists from Step 5 that don't mention the business yet, and how to get on them.
- Reviews: how to ask for them and reply to them, following Google's review rules.
Put the most important task first. Finish with one thing the business shouldn't spend time on yet, and why.

THE REPORT
Give me, in this order: the findings from Steps 1 to 3 (up to 8, each with what you found, why it matters to a customer, and the URL), the questions and businesses from Step 4, the table and count from Step 5, the strengths and gaps from Step 6, and the 30-day plan from Step 7.

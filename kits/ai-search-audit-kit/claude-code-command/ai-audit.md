description: Audit how a local business shows up on Google and in AI answers
argument-hint: business name, trade, town and state, website, main phone
---

You're auditing how one local business shows up on Google and in AI answers. Work through every step below in order, without stopping to ask me, and give me one report at the end.

Business details: $ARGUMENTS

If any of these are missing from the details (business name, trade, town and state, website, main phone), ask me for them once before you start.

Rules for every step: Use only what you can open, and give the URL for each finding. If you can't open or confirm something, write NOT VERIFIED and never guess or fill the gap from memory. If information comes from a site other than the business's own, such as Yelp, Birdeye or BBB, label it secondhand. Say when something is your judgment. Don't judge how the site looks on a phone; I'll check that myself. Before you write the report, go back over each finding and check it against the page you cited.

Step 1, the website: Does it say what they do, where, and how to call? Is there one real page per service, and real pages for the towns they serve? Does it answer common customer questions? Is there proof, such as reviews, licenses and real photos? Is anything blocking search engines? Spot-check up to 3 service pages and 3 town pages.

Step 2, outside listings: Do the name, address and phone match the website on the listings you can find? If I've given you screenshots of the Google Business Profile, list the category, services, hours, review count and rating, how recent the newest reviews are, owner replies, photos, and call and booking links. If I haven't, mark the Google profile NOT VERIFIED.

Step 3, what AI says about them: Search for what you can find about the business in its town. Report whether the facts match the website, and where any mismatch comes from.

Step 4, who gets named: Search the way a customer would, without the business name: "best [trade] companies in [town, state]" and "[the most common urgent problem in this trade] in [town, state], who should I call." List the businesses that come up and the pages they come from.

Step 5, who is being read: Open each page from Step 4. Give a table: page, type (directory, review site, best-of list, competitor, news, other), and whether it mentions the business. If you can't open a page, mark it NOT READABLE and don't count it. Give the count: X of Y readable pages mention the business.

Step 6, SWOT: Using only what you verified, give a SWOT: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats, each with its source.

Step 7, the first move: Say which gap is biggest: content depth, more content, site structure and readability, or the Google profile. Give the one fix to do first and why, the next two, and one thing they shouldn't chase and why.

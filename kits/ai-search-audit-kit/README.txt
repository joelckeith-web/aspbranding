AI SEARCH AUDIT KIT
From the AI for Founders x ASP live audit workshop

This folder has everything we used in the workshop, so you can run the same audit on your own business.

WHAT'S INSIDE

1-ai-search-audit.txt
  The one-file audit. Fill in your business details at the top, paste the whole thing into Claude with web search on, and it works through every step in order.

2-prompts-from-the-show.txt
  The separate prompts we used along the way: the Google profile screenshot prompt, the three AI questions, and two prompts to use every week.

3-free-tools.txt
  The free tools we used, with links.

4-your-next-30-days.txt
  The four-week plan from the end of the workshop.

claude-code-command/ai-audit.md
  The same audit as a slash command, for anyone who uses Claude Code. Put it in your project's .claude/commands folder, then type /ai-audit followed by your business details.

BEFORE YOU START

- Use Claude with web search on, in a new chat.
- Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity in a temporary or logged-out chat, so your history doesn't shape the answer.
- Run each question more than once. AI answers change from run to run, so look for the pattern.
- Check what comes back. Claude makes mistakes and can fill in details on its own, so open the links it gives you before you act on anything.
- Nobody can guarantee your business a spot in an AI answer.

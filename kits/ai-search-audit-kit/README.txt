AI SEARCH AUDIT KIT
From the AI for Founders x ASP live audit workshop

This folder has a simple structure you can use to audit how your own business shows up on Google and in AI answers, and to turn what you find into a 30-day plan.

WHAT'S INSIDE

1-ai-search-audit.txt
  One prompt that runs everything, step by step. Fill in your business details at the top, paste it into Claude with web search on, and it runs the audit first, then writes a 30-day plan for your business. There's nothing to paste in between.

2-free-tools.txt
  The free tools we used, with links.

claude-code-command/ai-audit.md
  The same prompt as a slash command, for anyone who uses Claude Code. Put it in your project's .claude/commands folder, then type /ai-audit followed by your business details.

BEFORE YOU START

- Use Claude with web search on, in a new chat.
- Check what comes back. Claude makes mistakes and can fill in details on its own, so open the links it gives you before you act on anything.
- If Claude stops partway, type "keep going."
- AI answers change from run to run. Run the audit again in a few weeks and look for the pattern.
- Nobody can guarantee your business a spot in an AI answer.

CHECK YOUR SITE ON YOUR PHONE

The audit leaves this part to you, because Claude can't see how your site looks in your hand.
- Can someone tell what you do, where, and how to call in five seconds?
- Is the call button easy to tap?
- Is there one clear next step?
- Run PageSpeed Insights and the Rich Results Test (links in 2-free-tools.txt).

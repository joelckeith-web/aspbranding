## What a Home Service Marketing Audit Checks

During the 60-minute call, we walk through every part of your marketing system, not only one piece of it. That includes:

- **Google Ads** — waste, bid strategy, and campaign structure
- **Local SEO and your Google Business Profile** — how you rank and show up for the searches that matter
- **Organic search and how newer search assistants, like ChatGPT, understand your business**
- **Meta, email, content, and social media**
- **Your website's conversion path** — what happens after someone lands on your site
- **Tracking** — whether your CRM can tell you which ad drove which job

Most home service businesses have pieces of this in place but no full picture. You might run [local SEO](/blog/local-seo-for-home-service-businesses) campaigns without knowing if your [Google Business Profile](/blog/gbp-vs-website-home-service-priority) is pulling its weight, or spend on Google Ads without a clear read on cost per booked job. The audit pulls every channel into one view so you can see where the gaps are.

Search behavior is also shifting. Homeowners increasingly ask chat-based tools for contractor recommendations before they ever open Google. If your business isn't set up to show up in those answers, that's a growing blind spot worth checking. We cover this during the [answer engine optimization](/blog/ai-optimization-aeo-home-service-businesses-2025) portion of the audit.

**Key Takeaway:** A real marketing audit looks at your ads, SEO, website, and tracking together, not one channel in isolation. Fixing one piece while ignoring the rest rarely moves revenue.

## Why Most Home Service Businesses Can't See Where Their Leads Come From

Here's the part that surprises most owners: even with a CRM like Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ServiceTitan running in the background, most shops still can't answer a simple question. Which ad, which keyword, or which review turned into a booked job?

ASP is an Official Housecall Pro Affiliate Partner, and one of the most common issues we find during audits is a tracking gap between marketing spend and job data. Without that connection, you're guessing at what's working, even if the dashboards look busy. This is part of why we built out a full breakdown of [marketing attribution for home service businesses](/blog/marketing-attribution-home-service-businesses), because it's one of the biggest blind spots in the industry.

Keeping your CRM data accurate and secure matters as much as tracking it correctly, which is why [field software data security](/blog/field-software-data-security) belongs in the same conversation as attribution.

**Key Takeaway:** Spending on marketing without tracking it back to booked jobs means you're flying blind. Fixing attribution often unlocks more revenue than adding new ad spend.

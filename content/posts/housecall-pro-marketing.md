Housecall Pro marketing covers two things: the marketing tools built into Housecall Pro, and the work you add around them to win new customers and prove which ads pay. HCP's own tools send postcards, emails, job reminders, follow-up texts and review requests to customers already in your system. Bringing in new customers through SEO and ads, and tying each booked job back to the ad behind it, is the part you add around them.

This guide walks through what HCP does on its own, where it stops, and how to connect it to Google Ads, Local Services Ads and your reporting. It relies on Housecall Pro's own feature pages and help articles, checked in October 2026, plus how we set up HCP at ASP. ASP is a Housecall Pro Affiliate, and our [Housecall Pro marketing page](/housecall-pro) gives the short version.

## What Housecall Pro's Marketing Tools Do

HCP's [automated marketing](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/automated-marketing/) page says the tools are there to "drive more repeat business and referrals." Here is what the software handles by itself, according to HCP's feature pages and help center:

- **Automated marketing:** postcards, including one-time or recurring campaigns to customer segments, plus email marketing, reminder emails before each job and thank-you emails after.
- **Automated Text Campaigns:** texts to up to 10 customers a day who are due for follow-up, with messages you can review, edit, skip or turn off.
- **[Review management](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/review-management-software/):** automated review requests after each job or payment, and one dashboard to view and respond to Google and Facebook reviews.
- **[Lead Source field](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5434315-lead-source-overview):** records where a customer found you, on customers, jobs and estimates.
- **[Reporting](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/advanced-reporting/):** custom reports filtered by job status, customer and lead source, plus Analyst AI, which builds reports from a typed prompt.
- **[Online Booking](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/online-booking/):** bookable time slots on your website, social media and Google, based on your team's schedule, service area and job duration.
- **[CSR AI](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/csr-ai/):** answers calls and website chats and books jobs from your account's services and availability.

Some of these features may depend on your HCP plan. We did not confirm availability plan by plan, so check what your own account includes.

**Key Takeaway:** HCP already covers reminders, review requests, postcards, emails, booking and lead-source reporting for customers in your system, so build on those tools rather than replacing them.

## Where Housecall Pro's Tools Stop

Most of Housecall Pro's marketing tools work once a customer is in your system. Online Booking, CSR AI and the Local Services Ads connection help with new leads, but the outreach tools are aimed at customers you already have. Bringing in new homeowners takes SEO, a Google Business Profile, Google Ads and Meta ads, and HCP's automated marketing page does not describe running any of those.

The Lead Source field shows the gap clearly. HCP gives you the field and reports on it, but somebody has to fill it in correctly on every job. When a dispatcher picks from a list, "Google" could mean a search ad, a Local Services ad, the map listing or an organic page. Call tracking numbers and form fields that capture the campaign solve that, because the source is recorded automatically when the call or form comes in.

Reporting works the same way. HCP's reports show jobs, estimates and lead sources inside the software. To judge your marketing, those numbers have to sit next to ad spend, which lives in Google and Meta. At ASP, that is a monthly review that reports cost per booked job by channel.

**Key Takeaway:** HCP holds the customer, the job and the revenue, while ads, search and tracking sit outside it, so the work is connecting the two.

## Housecall Pro and Google Ads

When we checked Housecall Pro's [integrations page](https://www.housecallpro.com/integrations/) in October 2026, it did not list a native Google Ads or Google Analytics integration. It does list Google Local Services, CallRail for call and web-form tracking, Mailchimp, Zapier, Angi and Yelp. So if you run Google Ads, the link between a click and a booked job has to be built.

Here is how that link works. When a lead comes from Google Ads, Google attaches a click ID. That ID is captured on the website form or the tracked call and stored on the HCP customer record. After the job closes, the click ID and the revenue go back to Google as an offline conversion. Google can then bid toward leads that become booked jobs instead of raw leads.

For call tracking, we use CallRail or WhatConverts with most clients. The platform matters less than the discipline around it. Every campaign needs its own tag, and the tracking numbers need an audit every quarter, because staff change, campaigns get added and numbers get reassigned.

**Key Takeaway:** With no native Google Ads integration listed, closing the loop means capturing the click ID, storing it in HCP and sending booked revenue back as an offline conversion.

## Local Services Ads, Reserve with Google and Online Booking

Housecall Pro [connects to Google Local Services Ads and Reserve with Google](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/10450490-how-to-connect-and-manage-google-bookings-with-housecall-pro). Google bookings land in one Google channel in Job Inbox, and customers who click "Book Online" on Local Services Ads or Reserve with Google go to HCP's Online Booking flow instead of Google's. HCP says you can connect your LSA account to Job Inbox at no extra charge, but you must have your own Local Services Ads account to do it.

The connection handles the booking. It does not manage the ad. Local Services Ads charge per lead and rank on review volume, response time and dispute rate, and at ASP we manage the LSA account against those signals. HCP's automatic review requests help here, since every finished job is a chance to ask.

Keep those requests inside Google's review policies and the Federal Trade Commission's rule on fake reviews and testimonials. That means no fake reviews and no free or discounted services in exchange for a review, even when the request goes out automatically.

Online Booking adds another way in. Customers can pick from bookable time slots on your website, social media and Google, based on your team's schedule, service area and job duration. If you turn it on, check that online bookings carry the right Lead Source, so they show up in your lead-source reports.

**Key Takeaway:** HCP brings Google bookings into Job Inbox, but the LSA account still needs its own management, built on reviews and fast replies.

## Attribution From First Click to Booked Job

Attribution means connecting each booked job to the marketing that produced it. In a business running Housecall Pro, it rests on four connected pieces:

- Call tracking for phone leads.
- Website forms that capture campaign tags.
- Housecall Pro, holding the customer, the job and the revenue.
- The ad platforms, which need results sent back to them.

Most operators have three of the four in some form. What is usually missing is the connection between them: tracking numbers that are not tied to HCP, forms that drop the campaign tags, or a Lead Source field nobody fills in.

Repeat customers need their own rule. The first job is credited to the channel that brought the customer in. Every job after that is retention and gets credited to your follow-up, not to the original ad. Our [marketing attribution guide](/blog/marketing-attribution-home-service-businesses) covers the models and the monthly measures in more depth.

**Key Takeaway:** Connect call tracking, forms, HCP and the ad platforms so the Lead Source field fills from data and booked revenue flows back to the ads.

We set these tools up one at a time and confirm each one works before moving to the next. If you already use HCP, we start by auditing the configuration and fixing what is misaligned. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Housecall Pro do marketing for you?

In part. HCP's automated marketing sends postcards, email campaigns, job reminders and thank-you emails, and its review tools ask for a review after each job or payment. Those tools reach customers already in your system. Winning new customers through Google Ads, Meta ads and SEO is work you or an agency add on top.

### Does Housecall Pro integrate with Google Ads?

Not natively, based on HCP's own listings. Its integrations page, checked in October 2026, shows no native Google Ads or Google Analytics integration. You can still connect the two. Capture the Google click ID on your forms and tracked calls, store it on the HCP customer record, and send it back to Google with the job's revenue as an offline conversion.

### Can Housecall Pro show which ad produced a job?

Partly. The Lead Source field stores where each customer came from, and HCP's custom reports can filter on it. The report is only as good as the field. Filling it automatically takes call tracking and forms that capture the campaign, and sending booked revenue back to the ad platforms takes offline conversion setup.

### Does Housecall Pro work with Local Services Ads?

Yes. HCP connects to Google Local Services Ads and Reserve with Google, and Google bookings arrive in a single Google channel in Job Inbox. To connect it, HCP requires an account with Google Local Services Ads. The connection handles bookings, while the ad account itself still needs managing for reviews, response time and disputes.

### Which call tracking works with Housecall Pro?

HCP's integrations page includes CallRail for call and web-form tracking. At ASP we use CallRail or WhatConverts with most clients. The choice of platform matters less than the habits around it: a separate number for each marketing source, campaign tags on every link, and a quarterly audit of the numbers.
